Settings

Light, dark or system theme for every page, your xAI key and model, and where Scribe saves recordings.

Applies across the whole portal

How the portal works

Step 1

Start it with start.py

Double-click start.py in the portal folder, or run python3 start.py. It opens the portal and runs Scribe. Leave its window open while you study.

Step 2

Pick a module

Read one section at a time. Each one says which lecture slides it comes from, so you can switch between the two.

Step 3

Record with Scribe

Lines appear as they’re spoken and stay on this computer, ready to search, rename and export.

Step 4

Make it yours

Open Settings in the top bar to switch between light and dark, add your xAI key and choose where recordings are saved.
